## Overall Impression

The restraint is real and the machine layer is genuinely good — but the page treats a five-year archive as a list of thirty things. Everything that makes f/64 specific is in the data and not on the page: venues, ordinals, the year spine, the 33-month silence, the credits. The fix needs no JavaScript and no new mechanism.

## What's Working

1. **The closing statement is exactly right, and rare.** "El ciclo concluyó y no habrá nuevas sesiones." No mourning, no eulogy, no newsletter capture.
2. **The whole card is one link, and the machine layer is honest.** Full-card touch target; every date carries `<time datetime>` from the derived ISO value; CollectionPage JSON-LD emits a 30-item ItemList with positions.
3. **The restraint is genuine, not absence.** Thirty photographers in wildly different registers sit together without the interface adding a competing note.

## Priority Issues

### [P1] The opening sentence is contradicted by the register beneath it
src/eventos.md:13 says "cada primer jueves del mes." VERIFIED against all 30 derived ISO dates: 8 of 30 were not first Thursdays — 2023-10-12, 2023-07-13, 2023-06-08, 2020-01-09 (second Thursdays); 2023-04-05, 2023-02-01, 2018-12-12 (Wednesdays); and 2018-11-08, the very first session (second Thursday). Only one of three Wednesdays is annotated.
Why: the differentiator is that this is THE verifiable record; the binding commitment is nothing fabricated. A researcher who spot-checks one date finds the summary wrong. Same sentence appears on the home page and /acerca-de/.
Fix: "cada mes, casi siempre el primer jueves". Annotate all three Wednesdays consistently or drop the lone one; iso_date_for can derive the weekday.
Command: /impeccable clarify

### [P1] The 33-month silence is invisible and reads as missing data
Card 13 is 1 dic 2022; card 14 is 5 mar 2020. VERIFIED: 33 months, the only gap over two months in the archive. Unmarked.
Why: an unexplained hole is indistinguishable from data loss, and this page's job is to look complete. Discards the archive's most human moment.
Fix: a plain line between the two years, from what the data supports only: "No hubo sesiones entre marzo de 2020 y diciembre de 2022." Gris Ficha, label scale. Don't invent a reason.
Command: /impeccable layout

### [P1] Thirty flat items with no scaffold, and no list semantics
src/eventos.md:21-46 renders 30 bare `<a>` into a div. No year headings, no ul/li, no session numbers, no anchors.
Why: simultaneously the largest cognitive-load failure and the largest accessibility loss. No "list, 30 items" announcement; no structure between h1 and thirty sibling h2s.
Fix: group by year from event_iso_date, h2 per year with id, ul per year, li per card, demote card headings to h3, add "Sesión NN". Static markup only.
Command: /impeccable layout

### [P2] Thirty copyrighted photographs with no credit line
Every cover is the presenter's own work per copyright: in events.yml; /eventos/ renders all 30 with no ©.
Why: "a visible credit may never be dropped from a layout" is binding. The adjacent h2 is ambiguous — author or subject?
Fix: add © per card in the specified style, or state it once unmissably in the intro.
Command: /impeccable harden

### [P2] Redundant alt text, and one cover breaks row alignment
(a) alt duplicates the link's own text — 60 redundant announcements, describing nothing about the photograph. (b) MEASURED: aniversario.jpg is 1200x498 (2.41:1) vs ~1.91:1 for all 30 others; that card's text stack sits higher than its row neighbours.
Fix: (a) alt="" or a real description. (b) re-crop to ~1.91:1, regenerate .webp, add aspect-[1200/628] object-cover.
Command: /impeccable audit

## Persona Red Flags

**Casey (Distracted Mobile User)** — the primary visitor. Four labels for one destination ("Ver las 30 sesiones" / "Eventos pasados" / "Eventos anteriores" / "Sesiones de f/64"). Four viewport-heights of preamble before the first photograph. Thirty single-column cards with no year marker, no back-to-top. The entire interactive vocabulary is dead on touch: group-hover:scale-105 and group-hover:text-sky-600 are the only feedback, no :active state, so a tap produces nothing until the next paint.

**Sam (Accessibility-Dependent User)** — the grid is not a list: 30 anchors in a plain div, no role="list", no count. Every card announces presenter and date twice. No focus styling on any of the 30 cards — group-hover: with no group-focus-visible: counterpart. Thirty consecutive tab stops with no skip-past-list. No prefers-reduced-motion guard on scale-105 x30 or scroll-smooth. Dates ~4.76:1 at 0.875rem uppercase.

**Ana (investigadora, derived from PRODUCT.md)** — the venue is in the data and not on the page; she must open all 30 pages to build a venue column. Cannot cite an ordinal though JSON-LD numbers all 30. Four date formats in the human layer. ## Questions to Consider

1. The system says "The Date Is A Headline Rule" out loud. Why is the date the smallest, palest element on the one page that IS the chronology? What if the card inverted — date at headline scale as the title?
2. The 33-month silence is the only event the archive doesn't record. What would it mean to treat the gap as an entry rather than a rendering problem?
3. The ordinals already exist in the YAML order and the JSON-LD ItemList. What is gained by withholding a number the page already computes?
4. If people arrive by name search and land deep, is this page's job "pick one of thirty" or "show me the shape of five years"? Only one of those is a grid.
