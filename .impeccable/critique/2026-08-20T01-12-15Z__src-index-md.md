## Priority Issues

### [P0] h-screen + sm:overflow-hidden silently destroys the best writing on the site
MEASURED at landscape phone (844x390): content 708px inside a 390px box, overflow-y hidden, 318px clipped, final paragraph 222px past the boundary — entirely invisible, no scrollbar, no cue. Screenshot captured. At 1280x720 clips 104px but that is bottom padding; text survives. Portrait phone unaffected (sm: does not apply).
Why: silent content loss invisible to automated checks because the text is in the DOM. Screen-reader users get all three paragraphs; sighted landscape users get one.
Fix: h-screen -> min-h-screen at src/index.md:93, or drop viewport height entirely — this section holds prose, not a photograph.

### [P0] The closure sentence fails contrast — measured
Sampled actual pixels behind the text by hiding glyphs: hero lead is white 18px regular over a brightest-region background of rgb(111,124,129) = 4.31:1, below the 4.5:1 AA floor. h1 passes (4.66 desktop / 5.04 mobile as large text). Closing band passes (8.06 / 12.23). Difference is the scrim: hero uses bg-gray-300 (Velo Claro), other bands bg-gray-600.

Fix: move the closure statement out of the hero into a flat white band below in text-skin-muted. Do not just bump type size.
